I'm a freelance health journalist in London. I am originally from the U.S. and now cover public health issues across Europe and the globe.

I've also been a freelance journalist in Denmark and the Netherlands, and a staff reporter at news outlets in Washington, D.C., and Brussels. I specialize in reported features on how political and business decisions shape people's health and lives. In 2025, I won the European Press Prize's migration journalism award.
